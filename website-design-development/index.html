Websites that support the business

Website design and development for businesses ready for their next customer.

The Harness Lab designs and engineers custom business websites, website redesigns and connected web applications. Your services, proof of work and customer intake belong in a fast, legible experience across phones, tablets and desktop monitors.

Build around what a buyer needs to decide

A commercial trade or service firm needs to show what it does, where it operates and how to request work without friction. A specialized software company — such as our live product surfaces for Title-Desk and Artificial Landman — needs to demonstrate real workflows, answer technical buyer questions and guide visitors into an interactive trial or teardown.

What a business website project includes

  • Clear information architecture: dedicated service pages, proof of work, buyer FAQs and direct enquiry or booking paths.
  • Mobile-first responsive craft: comfortable touch targets, zero horizontal overflow down to 320px screens and non-zooming mobile form inputs.
  • Search & AI engine foundations: pre-rendered crawlable HTML, canonical URLs, Schema.org JSON-LD, XML sitemaps and machine-readable reference manifests.
  • Connected intake: direct links to booking calendars, API integrations and workflow automation with graceful email fallback if a network request fails.
  • Full client ownership: your domain, repository and hosting accounts remain under your control from day one.

Website or full custom web application?

When customers need authenticated accounts, document uploads, shared workspaces or automated deliverables, we scope a custom business application alongside internal staff dashboards via custom internal tools. Technical buyers can inspect our verification discipline and Perihelion Loop architecture on theharnesslab.dev.

Website project questions

Can you redesign an existing business website without losing search visibility?

Yes. Bring your current URL, what needs to change, and the pages or business functions you want to retain. We audit existing URLs, redirects, structured data, and enquiry paths before defining the migration.

Will a new business website work cleanly on phones, tablets, and search engines?

Every site is tested across mobile, tablet, landscape, and desktop viewports with crawlable pre-rendered HTML, canonical URLs, structured Schema.org metadata, sitemaps, and accessible forms.

Who owns our domain, hosting account, and website code after launch?

You do. Your domain, hosting accounts, content, and private Git repository stay in your name with a clear publishing runbook.
