Landman title automation questions

How does TitleDesk calculate mineral ownership fractions without rounding drift?

Mineral ownership is computed using exact rational fractions in deterministic code rather than floating-point decimals. Whenever conveyances or reservations on a tract do not sum to unity, the exact fractional shortfall or overconveyance is flagged explicitly with links to the underlying deeds.

Does automated title workflow software replace a qualified landman or title attorney?

No. TitleDesk and Artificial Landman produce source-linked draft workbooks, not title opinions or certifications. Automated OCR, pre-run scope checks, and dual-examiner runsheet preparation organize evidence and preserve book-and-page source links so a qualified landman or title attorney makes the final title conclusions.

Can The Harness Lab customize workbook templates and intake pipelines for our brokerage?

Yes. We configure company-specific Excel workbook layouts, column conventions, curative checklists, and permitted storage or indexing integrations around a sanitized sample assignment.
