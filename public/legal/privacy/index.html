What we collect

Information you give us. When you use the project intake form we receive what you enter: your name, email address and company, and if you choose to provide them, your role, website or repository, the kind of project, urgency, who approves the work, where your systems run, your current tools, a description of the work, what breaks, what success looks like, how access could be arranged and a good time for a call. When you email or call us, we receive your message and contact details. When you book a call, the booking tool passes us your name, email address, the time you chose and anything you write in the booking form.

Information collected automatically. Like any website, ours are delivered by hosting providers that receive your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested and the time of the request, and keep short-term server logs. Our intake service uses your IP address to limit repeated submissions and block abuse. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or session-recording tools on these websites, and we do not set cookies of our own.

Information from third-party services the page loads. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the Cal.com booking tool from Cal.com. Your browser contacts those providers directly, so they receive your IP address and browser information, and Cal.com may use its own cookies or local storage to run its booking tool. Their privacy policies govern that information.

The intake form checks your text in your own browser for things that look like passwords or keys and warns you before you send it. Please do not send that kind of information anyway.

How we use it

  • to reply to you, understand your project, prepare a proposal and schedule calls;
  • to perform and manage work you engage us for, including invoicing and support;
  • to keep our websites and systems secure and to prevent spam and abuse;
  • to keep business records and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations; and
  • to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use what you send through the intake form to train AI models. We will not add you to a marketing mailing list without your consent, and any marketing email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only as needed with the service providers that run our business, each for its own role:

  • Cloudflare, which runs our intake service;
  • Resend, which delivers intake submissions to us by email;
  • our email providers (Namecheap Private Email and Google), which host our mailboxes;
  • GitHub, which hosts our websites;
  • Cal.com, which runs call booking; and
  • if you become a client, the payment, accounting and software providers we use to deliver and bill the work.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights, property or safety or that of others, or as part of a merger, sale, financing or reorganisation of our business, in which case the information remains subject to this policy's protections. We share information for any other purpose only with your consent.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including the life of any client relationship and the periods our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations require. After that we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you. Copies in backups are deleted on the backups' normal schedule.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections and access limited to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Email in particular is not a secure channel for sensitive information.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to give you a copy, or to delete it, and you can opt out of any marketing email. Write to sales@theharnesslab.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may need to confirm your identity before acting, and we may keep information the law requires or allows us to keep.

Depending on where you live, state privacy laws, including the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, may give you additional rights. We will respond to requests as those laws require. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with "Appeal" in the subject line, and we will tell you the result of the appeal in writing.

Children

Our websites and services are for businesses and adults. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you contact us from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be processed in the United States.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we will tell you before it applies to that information.

Privacy questions and requests: sales@theharnesslab.com, or by mail to The Harness Lab LLC, 5900 Balcones Drive, Suite 100, Austin, Texas 78731.
