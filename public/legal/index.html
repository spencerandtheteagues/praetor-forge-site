Names and products we operate

The Harness Lab LLC does business as The Harness Lab and sells the following software products under its own name. Every customer uses the same software, paid per run or by subscription:

  • The Harness Lab — the company that builds and sells the products below. Spencer Teague, in San Angelo, Texas, wrote the software. The Harness Lab LLC owns the code and IP (theharnesslab.com). Engineering notes are published at theharnesslab.dev.
  • Title-Desk and Title-Desk Agent (the Title-Desk desktop app) — landman title research software. Upload or request county records. It reads the documents, builds the chain of title and runsheet, resolves mineral ownership and computes net mineral acres. The desktop app is sold by subscription. The hosted web app is paid per run (title-desk.com and desktop.title-desk.com).
  • Artificial Landman — a hosted Texas mineral title examination platform for land teams, brokerages and operators (artificiallandman.io).
  • AI Landman — remote Texas oil and gas mineral title examination across all 254 Texas counties supporting uploaded records and full-service county clerk retrieval (ailandman.io).

When you deal with any of these names, you are dealing with The Harness Lab LLC.

No legal, title or professional advice

The Harness Lab LLC is a software company. It is not a law firm, an abstract company, a title insurance company, a surveyor or an accounting firm. Nothing we publish or deliver is legal, title, tax, accounting, investment or other professional advice, and nothing we deliver is a title opinion, an abstract of title or a survey. Work that requires a licensed professional must be reviewed by one.
