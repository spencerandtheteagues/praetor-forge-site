Products for sale

Mineral title products. One product line, every customer.

The Harness Lab builds and sells automation products for the oil and gas land and mineral title industry. Every customer uses the same software, paid per run or by subscription.

The same software

Title-Desk, Title-Desk Agent, Artificial Landman and AI Landman are the products. Spencer Teague wrote them. The Harness Lab LLC owns the code and IP.

Title-Desk

Title-Desk is landman title research software. Upload or request county records. It reads the documents, builds the chain of title and runsheet, resolves mineral ownership and computes net mineral acres. Title-Desk Agent is the Title-Desk desktop app, sold by subscription. The hosted web app is paid per run.

Artificial Landman and AI Landman

Artificial Landman is hosted Texas mineral title examination for land teams, brokerages and operators. AI Landman is the hosted landman title product on the same platform.

Who wrote it, and who owns it

Spencer Teague, in San Angelo, Texas, wrote the software. The Harness Lab LLC owns the code and IP. Every customer uses the same products.
