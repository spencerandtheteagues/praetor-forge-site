Custom software development around the way your business works.

The Harness Lab builds websites, full-stack business applications and AI integrations. We start with the work your team needs to complete and design the interface, data model and integrations together.

Build the application around a real operating problem

A customer portal, document workspace or internal dashboard is useful when it removes a specific bottleneck. We look at who uses it, what information they need, what they are allowed to change and what a successful handoff looks like.

That context guides the build: forms and navigation, database structure, external service connections, reports and the checks that show the workflow is working. A clear scope is more useful than a long list of technologies without a defined result.

Connect the interface to the work behind it

  • Websites: clear information, accessible navigation and a path to a relevant enquiry.
  • Business applications: structured records, team workflows, dashboards and report preparation.
  • Integrations: connect permitted APIs and existing tools so information does not have to be re-entered at every step.
  • AI-assisted systems: add document interpretation and proposed actions within a defined review process.

We separate important external actions from drafts and proposals. A payment, publication or customer delivery needs different permission and recovery handling from reading a document.

Specialized software for specialized work

TitleDesk Agent is our desktop application for landman title research. It brings records, OCR, runsheets and report preparation into a workspace designed around the assignment. Read more about landman title workflow automation and company-specific integrations.

For other domains, the same starting point applies: bring the actual input, the desired output and the edge cases your team spends time resolving. We scope the product around those requirements rather than making every business fit one generic template.

Start with a focused project brief

Tell us who will use the application, which systems it needs to connect to and what work is manual today. An example screen or empty report template often makes the conversation more concrete. Do not include passwords or confidential records in the initial enquiry.
